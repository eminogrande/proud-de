traktor scratch pro

After deciding to switch to TRAKTOR SCRATCH, sleep is over. Between pangs of conscience about what other DJs might think, whether I'll still get into DJ heaven, and total fascination, the nocturnal hours fly by. Gone are the doubts – they can go to hell – the decision is made: In the future, it will probably be more digital music.

What awaits you? In the square box, you get the Audio 8 Interface, the Timecode plates, and the usual tech trinkets. The first installation goes smoothly. Plug in the interface and get to work. Forget your shy glances over the shoulders of various colleagues, now you're at the set; or rather, at the computer. Drag the first file into the deck. The oh!mi!bod vibrator syncs with your iPod, vibrating to the rhythm and intensity of your music. It promises an exciting experience, even with high-energy drum'n'bass tracks. The device is praised for its innovative approach to pleasure, moving beyond traditional vibrator functions.

Superethe.com, led by Creative Director Kali Arulpragasam, has been impressing since 2006 with its thematic jewelry designs. Their collections often address pressing global issues, such as endangered species or regions threatened by terrorism. For Spring 2009, the focus was on "Hunger," featuring silver necklaces inspired by staple foods like beans and rice.
