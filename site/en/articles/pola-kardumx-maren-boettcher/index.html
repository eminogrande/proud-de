Esmod Fashion meets professional photographer at Blumen-Sühr's basement.

What was planned was a really cool advertorial. Paid, editorial content. Bought checkbook journalism, just the way we like it at Proud. The magazines have to be paid for somehow, after all. The best thing is still to prostitute yourself and still do what you intended. We want a combo of the photographer and the designer. The article should introduce the photographer and present the designer. All Berlin natives, raised here.

Maren Böttcher, an infinitely talented photographer with a hot girlfriend in New York. Lively and occasionally couchsurfing in the Proud office. She is forced by harsh torture methods to regularly contribute pictures.

Pola Kardum, an Esmod student, creative designer, makes herself and her clothes available. Down to the basement of Blumen-Sühr, our trusted flower shop. Pola's flatmate, Emin's father, as well as one of our friends, together with the photographer's assistant, formed a perfect team. We are very satisfied with the result, but it did not fit the direction of the other marketing department. Even without financial injection, it is once again valuable to show the potential within us. You can also admire Pola's designer pieces on proudmagazine.de.
