The Ballhaus-Theater in Naunynstraße has been brought back to life after many years. No longer just a play every three months that no one hears about. Just recently, the video shoot for rapper Ceza from Fatih Akin's “Crossing the Bridge” took place there. The entire Naunynmafia gathered, curious like little boys, wanting to mess around, but behaved exceptionally well under the watchful eyes of Ibo from Naunynritze. It was probably the dolled-up hip-hop gurlz who acted like magnets on the crowd of extras until the early hours of the morning. Because that's all we saw of the shoot. The video will soon be available on MTV and YouTube. The drooling boys, however, remain in Naunynstraße. That's where you'll also find the Ballhaus. The venue is slowly becoming a filming and meeting point for creatives in Kreuzberg. If you want to know more about the entertaining theater program, then quickly visit their website.

Schnaps Magazine is a new video magazine for the "excess generation" in Berlin. It delves into the city's gritty and glamorous underbelly, showcasing its wild, sleepless nightlife. The magazine features everything from pajama parties with bands to uncensored party photos, reflecting Berlin's true, often intoxicated, soul.

Launch at Yaka Paka

proud #01 · Pages 16-16

A Bulgarian dance night at Yaka Paka in Kreuzberg, Berlin, transformed the venue into a vibrant techno playground. The event, organized with the help of a well-connected local, quickly filled with 500 guests. Attendees enjoyed the unique atmosphere, dancing on tables and interacting with the lively crowd, promising more Yaka Paka entertainment in the future.

très bonjour fashion show 2008

proud #01 · Pages 21-21

The Très Bonjour Fashion Show 2008 featured hot women in avant-garde latex couture lingerie and swimwear, captivating the proud editorial team. The event, held in a beautifully designed S-Bahn arch, showcased designer Viola Jaeger's latest collection and extravagant hat creations by Hat-Affairs. With stunning light design and projections, the "Deep Sea Geishas" underwater atmosphere impressed guests like Peaches and Estelle, who also attended the after-show party.
