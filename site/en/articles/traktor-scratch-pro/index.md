# traktor scratch pro

> Source: 01 proud issuu_output, page 48
> Issue date: January 2009

It really looks like what it is. And no, we're not giving away an MP3 CD with timecode beeps. True and true. A real four-deck TRAKTOR. Mafia contacts deep within the music industry made this possible, and we're only giving this unit away under the following four circumstances:

1) You send us a 20-minute tape mixed on vinyl + a video of you doing it, as proof.
2) You sign a declaration that you will never in your life, and as long as your TRAKTOR SCRATCH PRO has not been stolen, only play purchased MP3s.
3) Not yet determined at the time of going to press.
4) You manage to convince us in an email to win@[proudmagazine.de](https://proudmagazine.de) that we should disregard points 1, 2, and 3 and give you the thing for free.
