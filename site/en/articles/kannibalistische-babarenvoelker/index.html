pentagonik

proud #01 · Pages 24-25

Pentagonik, founded in 2005 by five friends in Berlin, started as a netlabel and party organizer, aiming to combine free electronic music distribution with vibrant events. They emphasize innovative projects, blending new music distribution methods with traditional media like vinyl and CD. The label has recently expanded into commercial music distribution with Pentagonik Digital, while maintaining its focus on musical quality and community engagement.
