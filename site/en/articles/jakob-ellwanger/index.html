proud #01 · January 2009 Page 38 1 min read
TL;DR

Jakob Ellwanger, despite an appreciation for futuristic industrial music, identifies as a "flower child" at heart. He discusses his musical influences, including Berlin's original sound and delicate floral themes. His work is associated with a sense of well-being, "Luft&Liebe," "Pentagonik," and "Nachtbanditen."
