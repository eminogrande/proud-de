oh!mi!bod

proud #01 · January 2009 Page 13 1 min read
TL;DR

The oh!mi!bod vibrator syncs with your iPod, vibrating to the rhythm and intensity of your music. It promises an exciting experience, even with high-energy drum'n'bass tracks. The device is praised for its innovative approach to pleasure, moving beyond traditional vibrator functions.

Languages
GermanEnglish
  • www.ohmibod.com

It's hard. It's made of plastic. 20 centimeters of pure bliss. This vibrator won't just give you a monotonous hum in three stages; it'll get you going with Madonna's "Vogue." Connected to your iPod, it vibrates in sync with the music, showing you more than just the vocal highs. Incidentally, its performance doesn't falter even with your favorite Drum'n'Bass song. Don't be shy, ladies, we at proud are totally down with it.
