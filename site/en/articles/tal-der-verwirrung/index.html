proud launch party

proud #01 · Page 62

The "proud launch party" was held on Saturday, January 17th, at the PHB-CLUB, featuring a lineup of local DJs. This event aimed to support local talent and was promoted on the procalmagazine.de website. Additionally, the Ballhaus Naunynstraße, a cultural venue in Berlin Kreuzberg, reopened on November 7, 2008, under the new artistic direction of Shermin Langhoff, offering innovative and diverse theatrical productions.

farbreiz

proud #01 · Page 62

The "farbreiz" event on January 10th at PHB-CLUB in Berlin promises an immersive blue experience. Everything from guests to decor will be blue, creating a unique atmosphere. Two floors will feature Electro and Funk music, with DJs including MS Elbe and Katovl Menovsky.
