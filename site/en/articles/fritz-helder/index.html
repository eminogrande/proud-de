fritz helder

Fritz Helder & The Phantoms, discovered on YouTube, deliver an infectious sound.

Berlin · January 2009 · Issue 01, Page 14 · 1 min read

View original page Markdown DeutschEnglish

Discovered on YouTube, where else? Fritz Helder & The Phantoms. Enticed by a super cool video for “Making A Scene,” we can't stop watching their live gigs. If you know and love Namosh, you'll go wild for Fritz Helder. The song is an earworm! Clearly, the show is absolutely paramount. So go celebrate and really rock out, guys and girls!

Superethe.com

proud #01 · Page 13

Superethe.com, led by Creative Director Kali Arulpragasam, has been impressing since 2006 with its thematic jewelry designs. Their collections often address pressing global issues, such as endangered species or regions threatened by terrorism. For Spring 2009, the focus was on "Hunger," featuring silver necklaces inspired by staple foods like beans and rice.
