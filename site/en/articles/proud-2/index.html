proud #01 · Pages 26-27

Demir, a DJ, shares humorous anecdotes and insights into his experiences, from playing a set while intoxicated to his approach to DJ changeovers. He discusses the perception of Berlin in the electronic music scene and reveals what he would do for his girlfriend, even if it means playing Schlager music. Demir also touches on his first DJ set and what he would never do abroad.
