très bonjour fashion show 2008

proud #01 · January 2009 Page 21 1 min read
TL;DR

The Très Bonjour Fashion Show 2008 featured hot women in avant-garde latex couture lingerie and swimwear, captivating the proud editorial team. The event, held in a beautifully designed S-Bahn arch, showcased designer Viola Jaeger's latest collection and extravagant hat creations by Hat-Affairs. With stunning light design and projections, the "Deep Sea Geishas" underwater atmosphere impressed guests like Peaches and Estelle, who also attended the after-show party.

Languages
GermanEnglish

An evening excursion is a fine thing. When it involves hot women in avant-garde latex couture lingerie and swimwear collections, it's enough to simply embrace the sweet existence. The proud editorial team found themselves joyfully celebrating in a lovingly designed S-Bahn arch, amidst endless rows of bikinis. With eyes and mouths wide open, we stared at the latest collection by young designer Viola Jaeger. The icing on the cake was the extravagant hat creations by Hat-Affairs from Munich, designed exclusively for this event. The "Deep Sea Geishas" underwater atmosphere was created by the incredibly cool lighting design (by Mark Beversdorf and projections by renowned 3D artist Till Nowak!). All guests, including Peaches and Estelle, also made an appearance at the after-show party hosted by Disko44. We expect great things from the extraordinary fashion label Très Bonjour in the future and look forward to exciting collaborations and the showroom opening on January 24th in Berlin. Need more proof besides our compelling pictures? Search for the keywords "Deep Sea Geishas" on vimeo.com.

pentagonik

proud #01 · Pages 24-25

Pentagonik, founded in 2005 by five friends in Berlin, started as a netlabel and party organizer, aiming to combine free electronic music distribution with vibrant events. They emphasize innovative projects, blending new music distribution methods with traditional media like vinyl and CD. The label has recently expanded into commercial music distribution with Pentagonik Digital, while maintaining its focus on musical quality and community engagement.

The Ballhaus Naunynstrasse theater has been revitalized, becoming a hub for creative activities in Kreuzberg.
