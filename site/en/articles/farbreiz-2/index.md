## TL;DR

The "farbreiz" event on January 10th at PHB-CLUB in Berlin promises an immersive blue experience. Everything from guests to decor will be blue, creating a unique atmosphere. Two floors will feature Electro and Funk music, with DJs including MS Elbe and Katovl Menovsky.

# farbreiz

> Source: 01 proud issuu_output, page 62
> Issue date: January 2009

SATURDAY, January 10th
PHB-CLUB
[Landsberger Allee 54]

MS Elbe (Femalepressure), Katovl Menovsky (proud), CanDJane (Pentagonik), Phillisight

Farbreiz. Blue. The name becomes the program: from the guests, to the lighting, to the decor, everything will be blue... Electro and Funk will be played on two floors.
