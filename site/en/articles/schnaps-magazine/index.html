Schnaps Magazine is a new video magazine for the "excess generation" in Berlin. It delves into the city's gritty and glamorous underbelly, showcasing its wild, sleepless nightlife. berlin's daily circus

Finally – the new video magazine for the excess generation.

Forget decency, high gloss, and apple spritzers! Berlin finally has a magazine that goes where other kids don't dare: into the dirty-glamorous depths of a city that has forgotten how to sleep.

Whether it's pajama parties with Killgoth bands, toilet interviews with rock stars, mind-cuddling with Rummelsnuff, self-experiments in your trusted SM club, fashion shoots with the dead, uncensored photo series from last night's party, or throwing bratwurst with porn stars – Schnaps brings us a new pearl of our beautiful neon world every day. And Berlin finally gets a diary that shows its soul as it is: dirty, beautiful, unexpected, excessive, and always a little drunk.

Because Berlin is many things – but one thing for sure: Schnaps.

Thank you, guys!
