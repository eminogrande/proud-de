Lyndon Wade really loves to photograph. Damn, he's a photographer. That's his life. After all, he gets money and recognition for it. And since he's really, really good at one thing, everyone wants to be like him. That's how it is with stars, including Lyndon.

Lyndon is one of our fresh connections, and we already owe him something. Because one evening, when we no longer felt like doing anything useful, we surfed through various design blogs, waiting for the muse of creativity and desire to work to inspire us anew and captivate us. The trance ended when we stumbled upon Lyndon's homepage. Enough with the fun. The pictures literally stood out to us. We continued working cheerfully and recharged, but his photos didn't let go of us even days later. We don't want to keep something as unique as Lyndon's pictures from you.

Lyndon is one of the top 15 photographers in America, crowned by the Eisner Museum, and enjoys photographing for very large global brands at a very good price. Luckily, Lyndon is a good guy, because he could have easily blown our budget for the rights to simply print his pictures. But he didn't. Instead, he wrote to us that he would love to see his pictures in proud. We are proud to introduce Lyndon Wade to you.
