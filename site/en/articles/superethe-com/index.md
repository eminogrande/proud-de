## TL;DR

Superethe.com, led by Creative Director Kali Arulpragasam, has been impressing since 2006 with its thematic jewelry designs. Their collections often address pressing global issues, such as endangered species or regions threatened by terrorism. For Spring 2009, the focus was on "Hunger," featuring silver necklaces inspired by staple foods like beans and rice.

Again and again and again and again. Creative Director Kali Arulpragasam has been impressing us since 2006. Thematic jewelry design. Knuckle dusters for endangered species or chest pieces for countries threatened by terrorist nests. For the budding Spring 2009: Hunger. Silver necklaces inspired by beans, rice, and other staple foods. Unfortunately, it hasn't arrived in Berlin yet, but a flight to London only costs a tenner.
