# Flash

> Flash: the stories printed under this rubric in proud magazine Berlin, issue 01 (January 2009).

- [world 2.1](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/world-2-1/index.md): What would the world be without science fiction? The right hemisphere of the brain loves personal profiles, MySpace, Facebook, and all the new connectivity.
- [five minutes review on](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/five-minutes-review-on/index.md): This review details the experience of switching to TRAKTOR SCRATCH PRO, highlighting the initial fascination and the transition to digital music. It covers the unboxing and installation process, noting the ease of setup. The article also touches on the Timecode plates and their various play lengths.
- [phb berlin](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/phb-berlin/index.md): phb berlin is an ambitious project creating a large cultural space for art, artists, and artworks, including over 100 studios and sound stages. It will feature areas for photography, film, music, and fashion, alongside an electronic club. The project is supported by proud, which will follow its development and feature its artists.
- [oh!mi!bod](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/oh-mi-bod/index.md): The oh!mi!bod vibrator syncs with your iPod, vibrating to the rhythm and intensity of your music. It promises an exciting experience, even with high-energy drum'n'bass tracks. The device is praised for its innovative approach to pleasure, moving beyond traditional vibrator functions.
- [Superethe.com](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/superethe-com/index.md): Superethe.com, led by Creative Director Kali Arulpragasam, has been impressing since 2006 with its thematic jewelry designs. Their collections often address pressing global issues, such as endangered species or regions threatened by terrorism. For Spring 2009, the focus was on "Hunger," featuring silver necklaces inspired by staple foods like beans and rice.
- [fritz helder](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/fritz-helder/index.md): Fritz Helder & The Phantoms, discovered on YouTube, deliver an infectious sound. Their track "Making A Scene" is a catchy earworm, accompanied by a fantastic video. Fans of Namosh will likely enjoy Fritz Helder's music and energetic live performances.
- [artgerechtes](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/artgerechtes/index.md): 14 students are challenging the stereotype that eco-friendly fashion is unfashionable. They offer stylish, fair-trade, and organically produced clothing. They also invite customers to submit their own design ideas to be printed on shirts.
- [e-rags](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/e-rags/index.md): Mariendorf. An old, gray factory hall.
- [mavi](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/mavi/index.md): Hong Kong. Hip party, we're invited – on the list.
- [très bonjour](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/tr-s-bonjour/index.md): Wondering how incredibly hot a woman can look in a blue metallic swimsuit? Then don't pass Go, never wear anything else again, and head straight to the report!
- [shit glitter](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/shit-glitter/index.md): Shit Glitter offers a unique product that allows you to "shit gold." For $450, customers receive a 14-karat gold capsule. This product is available through their website.
- [200 calories](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/200-calories/index.md): Hey Fatties, have you ever wondered what 200 calories actually look like? Here's an example...
