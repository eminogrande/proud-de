# Grabs

> Grabs: the stories printed under this rubric in proud magazine Berlin, issue 01 (January 2009).

- [traktor scratch pro](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/traktor-scratch-pro/index.md): It really looks like what it is. And no, we're not giving away an MP3 CD with timecode beeps.
- [6-rags](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/6-rags/index.md): 6-rags, a brand with its headquarters in Berlin, has quickly established nine stores in the city and is known for adorning Berlin's buses. They offer a fresh collection of jeans and sweaters, with some available as giveaways. Additionally, Rouge Bunny Rouge provides a premium make-up collection, highly praised by proud models, stylists, and visitors.
- [très bonjour](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/tr-s-bonjour-2/index.md): You've seen it. You want it.
- [naketan](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/naketan/index.md): The "naketan" collection, particularly the "Blondie" line, is highlighted for its comfort and appeal. Marketed with the slogan "favorite pieces," it aims to be a beloved choice for women. The article encourages women to enjoy the collection and men to embrace "TRAKTOR!"
