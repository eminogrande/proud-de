# Streets ahead

> Streets ahead: the stories printed under this rubric in proud magazine Berlin, issue 01 (January 2009).

- [love in berlin](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/love-in-berlin/index.md): Christian Rothenhagen, also known as deerBLN, is a Berlin-based artist who beautifies the city with his intricate tile designs. He is a trained cabinetmaker, social pedagogue, and professional graphic designer and illustrator. Rothenhagen discusses his motivations, his perspective on Berlin, and his recent gallery tour in California, emphasizing collaboration and his unique artistic approach.
