# Chat

> Chat: the stories printed under this rubric in proud magazine Berlin, issue 01 (January 2009).

- [pentagonik](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/pentagonik/index.md): Pentagonik, founded in 2005 by five friends in Berlin, started as a netlabel and party organizer, aiming to combine free electronic music distribution with vibrant events. They emphasize innovative projects, blending new music distribution methods with traditional media like vinyl and CD. The label has recently expanded into commercial music distribution with Pentagonik Digital, while maintaining its focus on musical quality and community engagement.
- [Other Directions](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/andere-richtungen/index.md): Demir, a DJ, shares humorous anecdotes and insights into his experiences, from playing a set while intoxicated to his approach to DJ changeovers. He discusses the perception of Berlin in the electronic music scene and reveals what he would do for his girlfriend, even if it means playing Schlager music. Demir also touches on his first DJ set and what he would never do abroad.
- [proud](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/proud-2/index.md): Damn. Yee!
- [dj craft](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/dj-craft/index.md): Play Technol And draw...
- [Cannibalistic Barbarian Peoples](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/kannibalistische-babarenvoelker/index.md): Jungle forests, screaming unicorns – what is Demir, producer and DJ of Pentagonik, doing outside of Berlin? This article from 01 proud issuu_output, pages 26-26, delves into the life and work of this intriguing artist, offering a glimpse into his world.
