# Report

> Report: the stories printed under this rubric in proud magazine Berlin, issue 01 (January 2009).

- [très bonjour fashion show 2008](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/tr-s-bonjour-fashion-show-2008/index.md): The Très Bonjour Fashion Show 2008 featured hot women in avant-garde latex couture lingerie and swimwear, captivating the proud editorial team. The event, held in a beautifully designed S-Bahn arch, showcased designer Viola Jaeger's latest collection and extravagant hat creations by Hat-Affairs. With stunning light design and projections, the "Deep Sea Geishas" underwater atmosphere impressed guests like Peaches and Estelle, who also attended the after-show party.
- [ceza at ballhaus naunynstrasse](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/ceza-at-ballhaus-naunynstrasse/index.md): The Ballhaus Naunynstrasse theater has been revitalized, becoming a hub for creative activities in Kreuzberg. Recently, it hosted the video shoot for rapper Ceza from Fatih Akin's film "Crossing the Bridge." The event attracted a large crowd, including local residents and hip-hop enthusiasts, eager to witness the production.
- [Launch at Yaka Paka](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/launch-at-yaka-paka/index.md): A Bulgarian dance night at Yaka Paka in Kreuzberg, Berlin, transformed the venue into a vibrant techno playground. The event, organized with the help of a well-connected local, quickly filled with 500 guests. Attendees enjoyed the unique atmosphere, dancing on tables and interacting with the lively crowd, promising more Yaka Paka entertainment in the future.
- [schnaps magazine](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/schnaps-magazine/index.md): Schnaps Magazine is a new video magazine for the "excess generation" in Berlin. It delves into the city's gritty and glamorous underbelly, showcasing its wild, sleepless nightlife. The magazine features everything from pajama parties with bands to uncensored party photos, reflecting Berlin's true, often intoxicated, soul.
