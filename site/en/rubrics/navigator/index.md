# Navigator

> Navigator: the stories printed under this rubric in proud magazine Berlin, issue 01 (January 2009).

- [proud launch party](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/proud-launch-party/index.md): The "proud launch party" was held on Saturday, January 17th, at the PHB-CLUB, featuring a lineup of local DJs. This event aimed to support local talent and was promoted on the procalmagazine.de website. Additionally, the Ballhaus Naunynstraße, a cultural venue in Berlin Kreuzberg, reopened on November 7, 2008, under the new artistic direction of Shermin Langhoff, offering innovative and diverse theatrical productions.
- [valley of confusion](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/tal-der-verwirrung/index.md): SATURDAY 24. January Ritterbutzke [Ritterstraße 24]
- [farbreiz](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/farbreiz-2/index.md): The "farbreiz" event on January 10th at PHB-CLUB in Berlin promises an immersive blue experience. Everything from guests to decor will be blue, creating a unique atmosphere. Two floors will feature Electro and Funk music, with DJs including MS Elbe and Katovl Menovsky.

- [showroom](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/showroom/index.md): This entry announces the opening of the Très Bonjour showroom and atelier in Berlin. Located at Torstrasse 3, the showroom offers fashion and design items. It provides contact information and opening hours for visitors.
