# Feature

> Feature: the stories printed under this rubric in proud magazine Berlin, issue 01 (January 2009).

- [keller](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/keller/index.md): White Men and Cannons
- [Why all this party scene anyway?](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/warum-ueberhaupt-diese-ganze/index.md): I already had allegedly meaningful things in my employee existence, but somehow it wasn't for me.
- [Berlin bedtime](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/berlin-bedtime/index.md): Once you live the night, you want more.
- [farbreiz](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/farbreiz/index.md): Farbreiz is an event concept where everything revolves around color, offering a unique experience each time with changing drinks, visuals, and even guest attire. It's more than just a techno party; the music itself has a colorful quality, and the guests are central to the party's success. This consistent and thoughtful approach to color and detail has made Farbreiz events consistently well-attended and distinct from other club nights.
- [Your name is everywhere. A few words about the scene?](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/deinen-name-sieht-man-ueberall-ein-paar-worte-zur-szene/index.md): The young, growing party community in Berlin is interesting, even if the older generation always grumbles and reacts arrogantly. But it's the Berlin old-timers who go to the big names, or very young people who have just moved here and only know the big names.
- [Aural Pleasure](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/hoervergnuegen/index.md): Colorful, lively, and captivating
- [jakob ellwanger](https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/en/articles/jakob-ellwanger/index.md): Jakob Ellwanger, despite an appreciation for futuristic industrial music, identifies as a "flower child" at heart. He discusses his musical influences, including Berlin's original sound and delicate floral themes. His work is associated with a sense of well-being, "Luft&Liebe," "Pentagonik," and "Nachtbanditen."
