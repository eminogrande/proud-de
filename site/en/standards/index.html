Editorial standards and archive policy

How the proud archive republishes printed articles, what is changed, and how errors are handled.

What this archive is

A faithful republication of articles that were printed in proud between 2009 and 2014. The printed issue is the reference. New articles are not written here.

What stays as printed

  • Headlines, rubrics, article text and credits are taken from the printed page.
  • Names are spelled as printed. Where the masthead spells a name differently, the author page says so.
  • Every article links to the scanned original pages, so readers can check the text against print.

What is added or changed

  • The text was read from the scans by OCR and then cleaned. OCR errors can remain.
  • Short decks and English versions are machine-generated. English pages say so and link to the German original.
  • Image cutouts on article pages are crops of the printed pages, marked "From the magazine". Photo credits are shown only where they are printed.
  • Bylines appear only where the issue printed a credit. Articles without a printed credit show no author.

Errors and rights

Factual or transcription errors are corrected against the printed page. How to report one, and how rights holders can ask for changes or removal: corrections and rights requests.
