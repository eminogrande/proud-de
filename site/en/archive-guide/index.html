Archive guide

How to read, search and cite the proud archive, for people and for software agents.

Reading

  • All articles and all issues.
  • Each article has the reading text, the scanned original pages and links to stories from the same issue.
  • Authors lists everyone with a printed credit.

Citing

Cite title, issue, page range and URL, for example: "love in berlin", proud #01, January 2009, pages 22-23, https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/articles/love-in-berlin/.

For software and AI agents

  • llms.txt and llms-full.txt: reading lists.
  • Every page has a Markdown twin: add index.md to the URL or send Accept: text/markdown.
  • JSON: /api/articles.json, /api/authors.json, OpenAPI.
  • Search: https://proud.xn--wp9h.tk/api/search?q=techno.
  • MCP: server card.
  • All access is read-only and needs no login.
